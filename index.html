Projects

    Meghan never seemed to stop moving. Between a full-time career, college courses, leadership responsibilities, and a growing creative business, there was always another project waiting. Most people would have been overwhelmed, but Meghan found energy in building things, whether it was a new process at work, a resin art design, or a website created one step at a time. By day, Meghan solved problems and helped people through work in the financial industry. By night, classrooms, craft tables, and GitHub projects competed for attention. While others were winding down, Meghan might be writing a college paper, designing a custom resin piece, or figuring out why a section of CSS wasn't behaving the way it should. The challenge wasn't finding something to do. The challenge was finding enough hours in the day to do it all. What kept Meghan going was a belief that growth never really ends. Every completed assignment, successful project, and lesson learned became another stepping stone toward bigger goals. Whether leading initiatives at work, creating something unique for customers, or earning a degree one class at a time, Meghan approached each challenge with determination, creativity, and just enough humor to enjoy the journey along the way.
